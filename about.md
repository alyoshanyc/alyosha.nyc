Alyosha is a photographer based in New York.

Portraits and documentary work, made in the studio and out on the street and the road, mostly on film. The pictures favor patience over spectacle: people met along the way, places caught in passing.

Available for commissions: portraits, editorial, events, and travel. For bookings, prints, and press, get in touch. When reaching out, please include the kind of shoot you have in mind, rough dates and location, and any references that help.
